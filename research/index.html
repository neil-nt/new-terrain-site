New Terrain research, 2026

Is AI showing up in your bottom line?

Conversations with leaders of independent agencies and consultancies in Scotland and the north of England.

Book a time

The question

How is AI changing your business, and is it turning into better margin and growth?

The context

Everyone's using AI. Almost no one has changed how their business runs and makes money.

Most teams are faster than a year ago, but few P&Ls show it: the time is absorbed by revisions, admin and over-servicing. Meanwhile, clients are asking where the savings are, and many are developing their own capability: arriving with their own first drafts, or understanding what goes into producing the work.

Our view

From our work with agencies, consultancies and service businesses, getting real value from AI takes more than adding tools to how you work today. It means rethinking how work is delivered, how it's priced, how you deliver new value to clients, and how the business runs. We're testing how widely that holds, so tell us if we're wrong.

What we'll talk about

  • What has changed, in your market and inside the business
  • The biggest challenge in the business right now, AI aside
  • Where the time AI saves actually goes, and whether it reaches the numbers
  • What clients are asking for on fees, and what they still clearly pay you for
  • Whether you've changed how the business operates, who's leading it and how you're resourcing it
  • How organised what you know is: processes, past work, data, and how much depends on a few senior people
  • How it looks from the leader's chair
  • What you're deciding to sell, and charge for, next

What it isn't

A tool audit, a maturity score or a sales call.

Who we'd like to speak to

Founders, MDs, COOs and partners of independent agencies, consultancies and creative service businesses with roughly 15–150 staff, in Scotland and the north of England. Well ahead with AI or barely started: both answers are useful.

What's involved

A 45-minute video call. No preparation, no forms.

What you get

  • The report, first. How your peers answered, before anything is published: whether AI is showing up in their numbers, how they're handling client fee pressure, who's leading the change and how they're resourcing it, and what they're selling next.
  • A private read-out. Half a page on how your answers compare with the group, plus a few questions worth considering.

Timing

Conversations run until 18 December 2026. The report goes to everyone who took part at the end of January 2027.

Who's asking, and why

New Terrain helps agencies, consultancies and creative service businesses change how they work so AI shows in the bottom line. We do this work commercially, so we're not neutral. We're running this research to gather wider data, and to share what we find with the people who took part.

There's no pitch on the call. If you'd like to talk about working together, that's a separate conversation, whenever you want it.

Everything is anonymised, and nothing is attributed to you or your business without your written agreement. Calls are recorded only with your permission, and recordings are deleted once written up.

Is this you?

You lead an independent agency, consultancy or creative service business with roughly 15–150 people, in Scotland or the north of England. Well ahead with AI or barely started, I'd like to hear from you.
